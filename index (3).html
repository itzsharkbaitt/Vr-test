PALMSHIFT

Push off glowing pads with your hands to launch yourself across the course.

Desktop mode uses WASD and mouse look.

On screen: click the scene to capture the mouse, look around, and move with WASD or the arrow keys. Your hands float in front of you; brush a lit pad to push off it.
In VR: touch a pad with a controller and swipe against it to launch. The dotted arc previews your flight.

VR won't start?
  • Quest or other standalone headset: WebXR needs the page on https. Put this file on a static host (Netlify Drop, GitHub Pages, etc.) and open that link in the headset's browser. A file saved on your PC or opened over plain http won't offer VR there.
  • PC headset: connect it and start its runtime (SteamVR, Meta Link, Windows Mixed Reality), then reload in Chrome or Edge.
  • No headset: use Play on screen, or try the Immersive Web Emulator browser extension to simulate one.
